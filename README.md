# blinkit-dashboard
Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard
📊 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, product categories, outlet characteristics, customer-facing metrics, and business trends.
The dashboard transforms grocery sales data into an interactive business intelligence report using KPI cards, slicers, donut charts, bar charts, a time-series visual, and a detailed outlet-level table.
🎯 Project Objective
The main objective of this project is to:
- Analyze overall sales performance and key KPIs.
- Compare sales across different outlet sizes and outlet location tiers.
- Understand product performance by item type.
- Analyze sales distribution by fat content.
- Compare outlet types using sales, item volume, average sales, and ratings.
- Study sales trends based on outlet establishment year.
- Provide interactive filtering for business analysis and decision-making.

📈 Dashboard Analysis
1. Sales by Outlet Size
The dashboard compares total sales across High, Medium, and Small outlet sizes:
Outlet Size	Total Sales	Share
High	248.99K	20.72%
Medium	507.90K	42.27%
Small	444.79K	37.01%


Observation: Medium-sized outlets contribute the largest share of sales in the dashboard, followed by Small and High outlet sizes.
2. Sales by Outlet Location Tier
Sales are also compared across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations. The dashboard shows:
Outlet Location	Total Sales
Tier 3	472.13K
Tier 2	393.15K
Tier 1	336.40K


Observation: Tier 3 locations show the highest total sales among the three location tiers displayed.

5. Fat Content Analysis
The dashboard segments sales/item performance by:
- Low Fat

The visual indicates that Regular products account for approximately 64.73% of the displayed distribution, while Low Fat accounts for approximately 35.27%.
This segmentation can be used to understand product mix and consumer purchasing patterns.
6. Fat Content by Outlet Location
A separate visual compares Low Fat vs Regular product distribution across outlet location tiers:
- Tier 1
- Tier 2
- Tier 3
This enables comparison of product mix across different outlet locations.
7. Outlet Establishment Year Trend
The dashboard includes a time-series analysis of sales by Outlet Establishment Year.
The visual shows variation in sales across establishment years, including a visible dip around one of the early-2010s years and relatively stable values around 0.13M for several later years.
This analysis can help understand how outlet age/establishment period relates to sales performance.
